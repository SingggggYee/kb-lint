# No Frontmatter Article

This article has no YAML frontmatter at all. The linter should flag this as an error since the title field is required in frontmatter.

It still has enough content to not be considered a thin article, with discussion of various topics including the importance of proper metadata in knowledge base articles.

## Why Frontmatter Matters

Frontmatter provides structured metadata that enables automated tooling, search indexing, and consistency checks. Without it, articles become harder to manage at scale.

## Common Fields

- **title**: The display title of the article
- **tags**: Categorization labels
- **created**: Date the article was written
- **confidence**: How well-established the information is
